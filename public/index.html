ABOUT THE LEDGER

Useful context, direct sources

AI companies

A growing directory of AI employers and contributor platforms. Open roles and contributor registration are different opportunities. Registration does not guarantee work. Each card includes the source and its last check.

Opening and closing dates

Every card shows both fields. “Not published” means an explicit date was not supplied. Announcement and discovery dates stay separate. Excerpt-extracted dates must be confirmed in the full terms.

FAFSA by state

The calendar covers 50 states and Washington, D.C. for 2027–28, with the federal cutoff shown separately. Priority and final dates are distinguished. Opening refers to the online federal FAFSA launch, not a separate state grant launch. Funding-dependent programs have no invented closing date.

Coverage and images

Public feeds and optional daily searches supply news. Company hiring sources can be rechecked. The directory is broad and editable; it cannot include every AI company. Images use official website previews where available and company-name illustrations otherwise. No endorsement is implied.

Your privacy

No tax returns, bank details or application documents are collected. Bookmarks stay on this device. Optional email uses your configured address. The slideshow has manual controls and pauses on hover or keyboard focus.
