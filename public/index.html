BEHIND THE BRIEFING

A wider view of opportunity

The Ledger brings money-related discoveries into one personal reading desk.

Two useful collections

Latest news gathers RSS headlines and, when configured, broader web-search discoveries. Explore programs is a directory of ongoing services, not a list of current vacancies.

Clear origins, useful context

Every card links to its source. Publication dates are shown only when supplied. Country labels describe a feed's focus, not your personal eligibility. “Official domain” identifies a known program or government domain; it does not confirm a payout or an open application.

Coverage and refresh

News uses 17 predefined discovery streams and a 30-minute server cache. The optional daily sweep uses up to 15 broader search queries. No service can find every offer on the internet or access private task dashboards.

Keep informed

Watch keywords and browser notifications work while this page is open. With Redis, a search key, and an email provider configured, Vercel's daily job can find new links and send a personal digest while you are away.

Your data

Bookmarks and browser preferences stay on this device. The site does not collect tax records, financial account details, or benefit applications. RSS and search providers receive the fixed discovery queries; email and cache providers process the configured digest.
